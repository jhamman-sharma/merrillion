Frequently Asked Questions

What does Merrillion do?

Merrillion is a hospitality and operations company. We work across four functions: Hospitality & Resident Services, Facility Management & Maintenance, Wellness & Care, and Specialised Operations for senior living and serviced residences.

Where is Merrillion coming?

Our flagship is in Vrindavan. Planned spiritual destinations include Katra, Varanasi, Rameshwaram and Tirupati; leisure destinations include Goa, Jim Corbett and Dehradun, with more emerging destinations to follow.

What is The White Butter Studio Residences?

The White Butter Studio Residences in Vrindavan is Merrillion's flagship, where Merrillion is the hospitality and operating partner.

What are Café Prana and Café Monsoon?

They are Merrillion's café brands. Café Prana is inspired by spirituality, wellness and Indian culture for our spiritual destinations. Café Monsoon is a relaxed café and lifestyle brand for our leisure destinations.

I own a property. How can I partner with Merrillion?

Send us an enquiry using the form below. Our team will contact you to understand your property and explain how Merrillion can operate and manage it.
